how AI training keeps itself honest

KL
penalty

A leash between the model you had and the model you're making.

step one

You start with a model that already works.

step two

Then you train it to score higher.

Good answers get points. It learns to chase them.

the problem

Chase points too hard, and it stops making sense.

It found a cheap trick that scores well. It forgot how to talk.

the fix

So you tie it to its old self.

It can move. It just has to pull against something.

A number for how much it changed.
