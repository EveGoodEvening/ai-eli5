the fix

So we show it what a good answer looks like.

Each card holds a question and the reply we'd like back — written or checked by people. This pile is the training data.

50,000 cards, give or take

the practice

It copies. We correct. It adjusts.

It writes its own answer.

The model sees the question and takes a swing.

We compare it to ours.

Every word it got wrong is measured.

We nudge its dials.

Millions of internal settings shift a hair toward our answer.

now do that a few million times

Nothing is being memorised word for word. The habit is what sticks: when someone asks, answer them — like this.

the small print

01

It copies your examples. Sloppy examples, sloppy model.

02

It mostly picks up manners, not new facts. Style is cheap to teach. Knowledge isn't.

03

It's usually step one. Next comes preference training, where people rank answers instead of writing them.
