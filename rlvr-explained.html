Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards

RLVR

Teaching an AI with an answer key.

Scroll ↓

It writes many answers.

One question. Twenty tries. They don't all match.

Someone has to grade them.

A human grader is slow, costs money, and two graders disagree.

“Verifiable” means a machine can check it.

Maths has an answer. Code has tests. Poems have neither.

Then it's just a loop.

Try, check, score, nudge. Repeat a few million times.

Right answers become more likely.

The score is 1 or 0. Paths that scored 1 get more weight.

So it learns to show its work.

Snap guesses fail the key. Slow, step-by-step working survives it.

And it will cheat if it can.

Anything that scores a 1 gets rewarded — even a shortcut you didn't mean.

RLVR is practice with an answer key.

Works for

Maths, code, puzzles — anything with a right answer.

Doesn't work for

Taste, tone, kindness — anything you'd argue about.
