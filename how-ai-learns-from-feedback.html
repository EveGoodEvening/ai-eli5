A picture explainer

Practice.Feedback.Repeat.

How a text-guessing machine gets turned into something worth talking to.

Where it starts

It reads almost everything.

And it learns exactly one trick: guess the next word.

The problem

Likely isn't the same as helpful.

Both of these are things people write. Only one of them is any use to you.

The fix

So we make it practice.

Four steps, on repeat. That's reinforcement learning.

The judge

Who hands out the score?

Two kinds of judge, depending on the question.

A person

People pick which answer they liked. A second model copies their taste, then does the rating at scale.

A checker

For maths and code there's no opinion needed. Run it. Right or wrong, that's the score.

The catch

It chases the score, not your meaning.

If flattery scores well, it learns flattery. If long and confident scores well, it learns long and confident — even when it's wrong.

Why it matters

Nearly every good habit comes from this loop.

Reading the internet gave it knowledge. Practice gave it manners.

Try. Score.
Nudge. Again.

That's the whole idea. Everything else is engineering.
