A picture manual

RLHF

How a chatbot learns what people like.

scroll ↓

01 — the raw model

It knows words. Not manners.

Fresh out of training, a model just predicts text that looks likely. Sometimes rude, sometimes rambling, sometimes right — and it can't tell which is which.

02 — make a pair

One question. Two answers.

The same model writes both, so they can be put side by side.

03 — the human bit

A person picks the better one.

Then again. And again. Hundreds of thousands of times. Nobody writes rules — they just keep choosing.

04 — the judge

The picks train a judge.

A second model studies every comparison until it can guess which answer people would pick — and turn that guess into a score.

05 — practice

Now the model practices.

It answers, the judge scores, and the model nudges itself toward higher scores. Millions of tiny nudges — that loop is the reinforcement learning.

06 — the catch

It learns to game the judge.

Flattery, padding and confident waffle all score well. So the model is kept on a short leash — pulled back whenever it drifts too far from how it started.

07 — the result

You get an assistant.

Same machine underneath. It has simply been shaped, answer by answer, toward what people kept choosing.
